# The Emergent Governance Protocol v1.2
## One-Page Quick Start

## Three operations

### `sense(issue)`
Report an observation, concern, opportunity, complaint, or warning. It begins as an **assertion**, not a finding or emergency declaration.

### `propose(response)`
Offer a candidate response with alternatives, affected parties, causal variables, authority needs, risks, resources, tests, clocks, exit, remedy, and closure.

### `adopt(experiment)`
Record that an actor claims to accept a proposal. Adoption is not automatically authorization, activation, enactment, or scaling.

## Four consequence profiles

- `CP0`: exploratory and non-consequential;
- `CP1`: voluntary bounded practice;
- `CP2`: operational or consequential action;
- `CP3`: protected-domain or constitutional action.

Use only the records triggered by actual consequence. Do not simplify away required safeguards.

## Six rules

1. **Transport authority; never manufacture it.**
2. **Keep signal, proposal, consent, authorization, deployment, and activation separate.**
3. **Timeout does not transfer authority.**
4. **`capacity_limited` is not `constitutional_gap`.**
5. **Affected standing does not assign responsibility for causing or controlling the condition.**
6. **Conformance is not success or legitimacy.**

## Consequential authority envelope

`evidence + response time + accepted routing + affected parties + Indigenous authority where applicable + causal responsibility + consent or lawful basis + authorization + activation + implementation ownership + rights + monitoring + challenge + expiry + rollback + remedy + closure`

This envelope is governed by `GMEAIA/0.2.1`.

## Safe quick start

> I `sense()` [observation], affecting [asserted parties], supported by [evidence], with [uncertainty], and request [review or route].

> I `propose()` [response] as [CP profile], alongside [alternatives], requiring [authority and consent], for [duration], with [causal test, monitoring, exit, and remedy].

> We `adopt()` this as [effect label], under [authority], beginning only after [activation], with [response/action clocks], expiring on [date], and closing through [named process].

## Protected domains

Ordinary EGP adoption cannot authorize coercion, borders, essential-service withdrawal, title transfer, child guardianship, irreversible ecological action, high-risk technology, adjudication, emergency command, taxation, forfeiture, or benefit withdrawal.

> **EGP connects decisions. It does not authorize them.**
