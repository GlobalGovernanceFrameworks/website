# European subsidiarity protocol
### An operational enhancement for Article 5 and the European project

**Status:** Revision 0.2 (Enhanced Draft)
**Target systems:** European Commission, European Central Bank (ECB), Committee of the Regions
**Parent frameworks:** Adaptive Universal Basic Income (AUBI), Financial Systems Framework, Nested Economies

## 1. Executive summary: Solving the latency crisis
The European Union represents the most advanced polycentric governance experiment in modern history, anchored constitutionally by the Principle of Subsidiarity (TEU Article 5). However, the EU is currently experiencing a severe structural latency crisis (as diagnosed by the Draghi Report on Competitiveness). The attempt to manage 27 diverse national economies through centralized, standardized regulation has triggered an **Averaging Problem**—stifling local innovation and generating massive bureaucratic friction.

The European Subsidiarity Protocol is not a political treaty; it is an **operational enhancement to subsidiarity implementation**. It provides the mathematical diagnostic tools (The Global Subsidiarity Index) to execute Article 5 accurately, and introduces specific routing protocols to unlock regional autonomy while preserving European interoperability.

**This protocol solves your biggest problems without threatening your power structure.**

## 2. The diagnostic engine: Operationalizing Article 5
Currently, the EU relies on political negotiation to determine if a decision should be made at the local, national, or Union level. This protocol introduces a rigorous engineering standard.

- **The Global Subsidiarity Index (GSI):** A diagnostic measurement tool deployed across the EU to identify the exact geographic and institutional scale where relevant information exists. The GSI will be co-developed with **Eurostat** for methodological transparency and validated through existing **Regulatory Impact Assessment** frameworks to ensure institutional trust. Data feeds are sourced from high-fidelity, real-time edge nodes (municipal registries, local economic activity) to prevent re-creation of the Averaging Problem.
- **Neutralizing the Averaging Problem:** By applying Ashby’s Law of Requisite Variety, the GSI mathematically identifies which EU regulations are actively destroying local economic variety, triggering an automatic devolution of decision-making power to **Regional Subsidiarity Zones** (RSZs)—formerly referred to as Bioregional Autonomous Zones—or local municipalities.

## 4. The European resilience dividend (Upgrading cohesion)
The EU currently expends massive resources on the Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) and structural cohesion funds, which often trap regions in bureaucratic dependency.

- **The upgrade:** Gradual redirection of existing structural funds into a European Resilience Dividend (a localized implementation of the AUBI Layer 1 baseline).
- **Cognitive infrastructure:** By decoupling citizen survival from rigid institutional compliance, the dividend serves as foundational cognitive infrastructure. It frees the intellectual bandwidth of European citizens, allowing them to innovate and adapt at the local level faster than centralized competitors (e.g., the U.S. or China).

**Tied to crisis:** Cost-of-living crisis, competitiveness gap.

## 5. The complementary digital euro (Routing local value)
To solve the investment and care deficits without violating the strict fiscal deficit rules of the Eurozone, the protocol introduces a **targeted fiscal complement within existing monetary architecture**.

- **The localized complementary mechanism:** Utilizing the GGF *Hearts & Leaves* architecture, regions are authorized to issue a Complementary Digital Euro. This localized currency is strictly routed toward regional care work, ecological restoration, and community resilience.
- **Explicit safeguards:** The Complementary Digital Euro is subject to **demurrage** (a holding fee that forces circulation) and is **non-exportable**. These mathematical constraints are presented as the primary safeguards that prevent this complement from ever threatening the ECB’s macroeconomic inflation targets. This is *not* a parallel currency—it is a bounded fiscal tool that lubricates local economies where the primary Euro has pooled or fled.

**Tied to crisis:** Investment gap, care deficit.

## 6. The European civic value registry (Solving the demographic crisis)
Europe is facing a catastrophic demographic shift, with an aging population and a shrinking tax base to fund traditional welfare state care.

- **The deployment of the Love Ledger:** The establishment of a decentralized, GDPR-compliant registry that recognizes and validates informal, non-monetized care work.
- **Explicit safeguards:** The registry operates under strict GDPR principles, with **user-owned data, local governance, and opt-in participation** to prevent surveillance or coercion risks. It is a voluntary civic infrastructure, not a mandatory system.
- **Structural compassion:** Instead of trying to commodify all eldercare and community support into taxable formal employment—which the system can no longer afford—the registry uses recognition mechanisms to build high-trust local social safety nets that operate outside strained municipal budgets.

**Tied to crisis:** Demographic collapse, eldercare funding gap.

## 7. Transition mechanics: The permeation strategy
The protocol does not require immediate, unanimous adoption by the European Parliament. It operates fully within existing legal mandates, requiring **no treaty changes for initial deployment**.

- **Municipal pilots:** The protocol is first deployed via the European Committee of the Regions and networks like Eurocities. Local municipalities adopt the diagnostic tools and Civic Value Registry under their existing legal mandates.
- **Pilot example:** **Västra Götaland (Sweden)** —a region with deep municipal autonomy (*kommunalt självstyre*) and zero secessionist tensions. Initial use cases include:
  - GSI applied to transport and logistics optimization
  - Complementary euro pilot for eldercare and informal care work recognition
  - Civic registry pilot for volunteer and community contributions
  - **Expected outcomes:** Measurable reduction in regulatory friction, increased care capacity without municipal budget strain, and a replicable template for other regions.
- **Inside-out upgrade:** As the localized nodes demonstrate measurable gains in efficiency, innovation, and social cohesion, the legacy hardware of the central EU apparatus naturally routes more resources toward these high-functioning polycentric networks.

This protocol positions Europe as the global leader in **adaptive, distributed resilience**.

- **EU model (with this protocol):** Adaptive + distributed + resilient—combining the coordination capacity of a union with the agility of bioregional governance.

This is Europe’s answer to the scale of the U.S. and the coordination of China, without sacrificing democratic legitimacy or local autonomy.
