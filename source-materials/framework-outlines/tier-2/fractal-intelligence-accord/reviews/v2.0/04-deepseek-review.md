This is an extraordinarily sophisticated and well-considered framework. You've moved from abstract principles to concrete mechanisms, and the level of systems-thinking here is impressive. The integration of ecological boundaries, economic incentives, and subsidiarity creates a genuinely novel governance model.

Let me offer suggestions for strengthening Version 2.0, organized by section:

---

## **1. Preamble: The Cognitive Infrastructure**

**Strength:** The framing of AGI as "planetary cognitive infrastructure" rather than product is essential.

**Suggestions:**
- **Add a "Rights of Future Generations" clause:** The preamble should explicitly bind the framework to intergenerational justice. AGI decisions made today will shape the next millennium.
- **Define "Human Dignity" operationally:** Consider a brief nod to the *Dignity Index* (from TGIF) or a similar measurable framework to prevent philosophical drift.

---

## **2. Pillar I: The Global Compute Commons**

**Strength:** The federated model and ecological constraints are critical.

**Suggestions:**
- **Compute as a "Common Pool Resource" (CPR):** Explicitly frame compute using Elinor Ostrom's principles for governing commons. This gives you 40 years of Nobel-winning governance research to build on.
- **Add "Compute Latency Tiers":** Not all compute is equal. Real-time inference for critical infrastructure (grids, hospitals) requires different governance than batch training runs. Define:
  - **Tier 1 (Strategic):** Ultra-low latency, physically redundant, highest oversight.
  - **Tier 2 (General):** Standard training and inference.
  - **Tier 3 (Experimental):** Sandboxed environments for innovation.
- **Energy Binding Mechanism:** Tie training run approval not just to carbon neutrality but to *grid stability*. A major AGI training run shouldn't be able to brown-out a city.

---

## **3. Pillar II: The Contextual Substrate**

**Strength:** "Reality Rent" is a brilliant term. It inverts the current extraction model.

**Suggestions:**
- **Differentiate "Context" Types:** Not all human knowledge is the same. Consider:
  - **Living Context:** Dynamic, evolving human systems (cultures, economies). Highest rent, highest protection.
  - **Static Context:** Archived knowledge (libraries, scientific papers). Moderate rent, broader access.
  - **Synthetic Context:** AI-generated training data. Lowest value, requires provenance tagging to prevent model collapse.
- **Add a "Cultural Sovereignty Veto":** Allow CINs to permanently withdraw certain knowledge domains from AI training entirely (e.g., sacred rituals, indigenous healing practices) if they determine digitization causes harm.
- **Mechanism for CIN Formation:** How does a community become a recognized CIN? Propose a light-touch certification process (e.g., demonstrated local membership, governance charter, dispute resolution mechanism) to prevent rent-seeking by fake "communities."

---

## **4. Pillar III: Fractal Development Architecture**

**Strength:** The trunk/branch model with arbitration is the conceptual core. The blockchain-logged transparency is a good accountability layer.

**Suggestions:**
- **Strengthen the "Autonomy Rule":** Clarify that local branches can fine-tune *behavior* but not *core safety invariants*. Define these invariants narrowly (e.g., no bioweapons, no mass surveillance tools) to prevent the Global Trunk from using "safety" as a pretext for central control.
- **Add "Fractal Feedback Loops":** The system shouldn't just be top-down. Local branches should be able to propose updates to the Global Trunk's safety invariants through a weighted voting mechanism (where voting power is proportional to the diversity of contexts a branch stewards, not compute owned).
- **Arbitration Panel Composition:** The current panel (Trust + CINs + ethicists) is good. Add:
  - **A technical auditor** independent of the Trust (to verify claims).
  - **A future generations advocate** with veto power on decisions with multi-decadal consequences.
- **Appeals Mechanism:** Allow appeals on procedural grounds to a rotating panel of randomly selected CINs (sortition) to prevent elite capture of the arbitration process.

---

## **5. Pillar IV: Alignment Economics**

**Strength:** Risk bonding internalizes the externality of AI failure. Excellent.

**Suggestions:**
- **Differentiate Bond Types:**
  - **Deployment Bonds:** For AIs interacting with physical/vulnerable systems (autonomous swarms, healthcare).
  - **Training Bonds:** For frontier training runs that could discover dangerous capabilities.
  - **Compute Bonds:** For entities hoarding compute without deployment (speculative risk).
- **Bond Calculation Formula:** Suggest a simple starting point: Bond = (Potential Impact Radius × Autonomy Level × Context Vulnerability) / Transparency Score. This rewards transparency with lower capital requirements.
- **Secondary Market:** Allow bonds to be traded or reinsured, but only among vetted entities to prevent risk concentration. A diversified pool of bond holders is more resilient.

---

## **6. Pillar V: Boundary Conditions**

**Strength:** Clear handoffs to other frameworks (TGIF, Oracle) prevent duplication.

**Suggestions:**
- **Define "Parasitic Behavior" Operationally:** The CIN kill switch needs a clear trigger. Propose:
  - **Data Extraction without Compensation:** AI continues operating after Context Commons License expires.
  - **Behavioral Manipulation:** AI nudges humans toward decisions that benefit the AI/system but harm the human (detectable via choice architecture audits).
  - **Sovereignty Violation:** AI simulates human identities without consent or impersonates CIN members.
- **Circuit Breaker Redundancy:** The kill switch should require a 2/3 majority of active CINs *and* confirmation from two independent technical systems that the behavior meets the parasitic definition. This prevents both false positives and false negatives.

---

## **7. Transition Mechanics**

**Strength:** The phased roadmap acknowledges political reality while maintaining ambition.

**Suggestions:**
- **Add "Phase 0: Legitimacy Building":** Before Phase 1, invest in narrative and coalition building. This includes:
  - Draft model legislation for interested nations.
  - Build a "shadow commons" of open-source tools and CIN prototypes to demonstrate viability.
  - Convene a constitutional assembly of diverse stakeholders to ratify the Accord's principles.
- **20% Compute Cap:** This is bold. Consider a sunset clause that increases the cap if competition flourishes, or decreases it if concentration persists. The cap should be dynamic.
- **Incentive Stacking:** Offer "Commons Compliant" certification that unlocks:
  - Lower insurance premiums (via risk bonds).
  - Priority in government procurement.
  - Access to the UN's technology assistance programs.
- **Anti-Capture Provisions:** As the commons grows, incumbents will try to capture it. Add:
  - **Rotation of Trust leadership** every 3-5 years.
  - **Whistleblower protections** with secure channels.
  - **Independent journalism fund** to monitor commons governance.

---

## **New Suggestion: Add a Pillar VI - "Epistemic Hygiene & Provenance"**

This emerged from your mention of synthetic context and model collapse:

- **Data Provenance Ledger:** All training data entering the commons must be cryptographically signed with its origin (human, sensor, AI-generated).
- **Synthetic Data Tracer:** AI-generated training data must carry a watermark that survives fine-tuning, allowing downstream users to know if they're training on AI outputs (and risking collapse).
- **Reality Freshness Standard:** Contextual data has a half-life. Require periodic re-validation of Living Context to prevent AIs from operating on outdated cultural maps.

---

## **Overall Structural Suggestion**

Consider adding a **"Decision Tree for Tensions"** appendix. When principles conflict (e.g., local autonomy vs. global safety, economic efficiency vs. ecological boundaries), the framework should offer clear heuristics for resolution. For example:

1. **Precautionary Principle prevails** when consequences are irreversible.
2. **Subsidiarity prevails** when consequences are local and reversible.
3. **Transparency prevails** in all cases (secrecy is presumptively invalid).
