# 🧪 ECB-Compatible Pilot Specification

### (Purpose-Bound Fiscal Instrument in Low-Transmission Sectors)

---

## 1. Pilot Framing (this is what makes it pass)

**Official classification (must be explicit):**

> “This pilot tests a **targeted fiscal transfer mechanism with embedded spending conditions** in sectors characterized by weak monetary policy transmission.
>
> The instrument is **not legal tender**, not a general unit of account, and not a store of value.”

This line is your **ECB entry ticket**.

---

## 2. Legal Positioning

Anchor it cleanly:

* Falls under **member state fiscal competence**
* Implemented via **Enhanced Cooperation (Article 20 TEU)**
* Consistent with **price stability mandate of the European Central Bank**

**Critical clause:**

> “The instrument is designed to operate outside aggregate monetary policy channels and does not target macroeconomic demand management.”

👉 This avoids triggering Article 127 concerns.

---

## 3. Scope Constraint (THIS is what calms the ECB)

You must make it **small, boring, and contained**.

### 📍 Geographic scope:

* Single pilot region:
  → Västra Götaland (Sweden) *(non-eurozone initially = even safer)*
  OR
  → Small eurozone region (e.g., parts of Portugal or Slovenia)

---

### 📊 Scale cap (non-negotiable):

> Total issuance ≤ **0.5% of regional GDP**

Even safer:
→ Start at **0.1%**

👉 This alone neutralizes:

* inflation fears
* systemic risk concerns

---

### 🧭 Sector restriction:

Only allowed in **low-price-elasticity, underutilized sectors**:

* eldercare (informal support)
* community health support
* volunteer coordination
* local ecological maintenance

**Explicit exclusion:**

* housing
* energy
* food retail
* tradable goods

👉 This prevents price spillovers.

---

## 4. Instrument Design (ECB-safe version of PBM)

We avoid calling it “money” operationally.

### Rename for pilot:

> **“Conditional Service Credits (CSC)”**

(Internally PBM, externally harmless)

---

### Properties:

| Property        | Specification                                |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Legal status    | Not legal tender                             |
| Convertibility  | Non-convertible to Euro                      |
| Transferability | Restricted (peer-to-peer within system only) |
| Expiry          | Yes (time-bound validity)                    |
| Storage         | Digital wallet (closed-loop system)          |

---

## 5. Demurrage (the sensitive part)

We keep it—but neutralize the threat.

### ECB-safe framing:

> “Time-based validity ensures circulation and prevents accumulation.”

Avoid the word *demurrage* in the pilot doc.

---

### Hard constraint:

* Fixed expiry: e.g. **90 days**
* No dynamic adjustment
* No policy lever

👉 This removes:

* “independent monetary policy” concern

---

## 6. Monetary Containment Mechanisms

This is where you win ECB trust.

---

### 🔒 1. Closed-loop system

* CSC can only be used within:

  * approved providers
  * defined services

👉 No leakage into general economy

---

### 🔒 2. No price formation role

> “Prices remain denominated in Euro. CSC acts only as a partial settlement mechanism.”

👉 This is HUGE.

You are not creating:

* a parallel pricing system
* a new unit of account

---

### 🔒 3. Euro primacy clause

> “All economic valuation remains anchored in the Euro.”

👉 This directly aligns with ECB doctrine.

---

## 7. Monetary Policy Neutrality Test (THIS impresses ECB)

You proactively define success criteria:

---

### 📉 Pilot must demonstrate:

* No measurable impact on:

  * regional CPI
  * wage levels in formal sector
  * Euro liquidity demand

---

### 📈 Positive signals:

* Increased activity in:

  * informal care
  * underutilized labor capacity

---

👉 You are speaking ECB language now.
