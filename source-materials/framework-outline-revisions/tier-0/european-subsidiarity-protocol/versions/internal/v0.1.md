# European subsidiarity protocol
### A cybernetic upgrade for Article 5 and the European project

**Status:** Revision 0.1 (Draft)
**Target systems:** European Commission, European Central Bank (ECB), Committee of the Regions
**Parent frameworks:** Adaptive Universal Basic Income (AUBI), Financial Systems Framework, Nested Economies

## 1. Executive summary: Solving the latency crisis
The European Union represents the most advanced polycentric governance experiment in modern history, anchored constitutionally by the Principle of Subsidiarity (TEU Article 5). However, the EU is currently experiencing a severe structural latency crisis (as diagnosed by the Draghi Report on Competitiveness). The attempt to manage 27 diverse national economies through centralized, standardized regulation has triggered an Averaging Problem—stifling local innovation and generating massive bureaucratic friction. 

The European Subsidiarity Protocol is not a political treaty; it is a cybernetic firmware update. It provides the mathematical diagnostic tools (The Global Subsidiarity Index) to execute Article 5 accurately, and introduces specific routing protocols to unlock regional autonomy while preserving European interoperability.

## 2. The diagnostic engine: Operationalizing Article 5
Currently, the EU relies on political negotiation to determine if a decision should be made at the local, national, or Union level. This protocol introduces a rigorous engineering standard.
* **The Global Subsidiarity Index (GSI):** A diagnostic measurement tool deployed across the EU to identify the exact geographic and institutional scale where relevant information exists.
* **Neutralizing the Averaging Problem:** By applying Ashby’s Law of Requisite Variety, the GSI mathematically identifies which EU regulations are actively destroying local economic variety, triggering an automatic devolution of decision-making power to Bioregional Autonomous Zones (BAZs) or local municipalities.

## 3. The European resilience dividend (Upgrading cohesion)
The EU currently expends massive resources on the Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) and structural cohesion funds, which often trap regions in bureaucratic dependency. 
* **The upgrade:** Gradual redirection of existing structural funds into a European Resilience Dividend (a localized implementation of the AUBI Layer 1 baseline).
* **Cognitive infrastructure:** By decoupling citizen survival from rigid institutional compliance, the dividend serves as foundational cognitive infrastructure. It frees the intellectual bandwidth of European citizens, allowing them to innovate and adapt at the local level faster than centralized competitors (e.g., the U.S. or China).

## 4. The complementary digital euro (Routing local value)
To solve the investment and care deficits without violating the strict fiscal deficit rules of the Eurozone, the protocol introduces a dual-currency architecture.
* **The localized complementary mechanism:** Utilizing the GGF *Hearts & Leaves* architecture, regions are authorized to issue a Complementary Digital Euro. This localized currency is strictly routed toward regional care work, ecological restoration, and community resilience.
* **ECB harmony:** Because the Complementary Digital Euro is subject to demurrage (a holding fee that forces circulation) and is non-exportable, it cannot trigger macro-inflation or threaten the European Central Bank's control over the primary fiat Euro. It simply lubricates the local economy where the primary Euro has pooled or fled.

## 5. The European civic value registry (Solving the demographic crisis)
Europe is facing a catastrophic demographic shift, with an aging population and a shrinking tax base to fund traditional welfare state care. 
* **The deployment of the Love Ledger:** The establishment of a decentralized, GDPR-compliant registry that recognizes and validates informal, non-monetized care work.
* **Structural compassion:** Instead of trying to commodify all eldercare and community support into taxable formal employment—which the system can no longer afford—the registry uses Gratitude Tokens to build high-trust local social safety nets that operate entirely outside the strained municipal budgets.

## 6. Transition mechanics: The permeation strategy
The protocol does not require immediate, unanimous adoption by the European Parliament. It utilizes a structural permeation strategy.
* **Municipal pilots:** The protocol is first deployed via the European Committee of the Regions and networks like Eurocities. Local municipalities (e.g., specific Swedish or Spanish regions) adopt the diagnostic tools and Civic Value Registry under their existing legal mandates.
* **Inside-out upgrade:** As the localized nodes demonstrate massive increases in efficiency, innovation, and social cohesion, the legacy hardware of the central EU apparatus naturally routes more resources toward these high-functioning polycentric networks.
