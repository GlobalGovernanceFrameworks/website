* **The Fix:** Do not delete it; **operationalize it**. Define "Ceremonial Governance" as the *mandatory Phase 1 Scoping Protocol* for any major infrastructure project.

### 2. The Missing Pillar: Freight and Logistics (The Cargo Blindspot)
Read the "Companion User Journey" in Section 6. It talks about a student using an e-bike, a farmer taking a shuttle, and a family relocating. It entirely focuses on moving *human bodies*. 
* **The Problem:** The vast majority of planetary transportation emissions and infrastructure wear-and-tear comes from **freight** (cargo ships, semi-trucks, supply chains). 
* **The Fix:** v8.0 must include a **Regenerative Freight & Logistics Protocol**. This should outline the transition to sail-freight for oceanic shipping, the electrification of rail-freight, and the implementation of "Cargo Bike Logistics Hubs" for last-mile urban delivery. If the framework doesn't govern how an Amazon package or a ton of grain moves, it isn't a planetary mobility framework.

### 3. The "De-Mobility" Mandate (The Right to Stay)
A truly regenerative transportation framework shouldn't just focus on making movement greener; it must focus on making movement *unnecessary*.
* **The Problem:** The framework assumes high mobility is always a net positive. But much of modern mobility is forced by terrible urban design (commuting 2 hours because you can't afford to live near work).
* **The Fix:** Introduce the principle of **Proximity Priority**. Explicitly tie this framework to the **Bioregional Polis** (15-minute cities).
