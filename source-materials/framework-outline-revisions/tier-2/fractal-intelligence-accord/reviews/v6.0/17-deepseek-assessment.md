### 1. The Fractal Intelligence Accord (v6.0)

**Assessment: Significant Room for Improvement**

This framework tackles the most ambitious and uncertain domain—AGI governance—with remarkable conceptual sophistication. Its "Texture Mandate" and framing of AGI as planetary cognitive infrastructure are genuinely original. However, the framework suffers from being a visionary philosophical document dressed as an implementation blueprint, creating a fundamental mismatch between ambition and operational detail.

**Key Issues:**
- **Premature Codification:** The framework is locked at "Final Revision" despite governing a technology whose capabilities and risks are evolving monthly. Many mechanisms (Contextual Intelligence Nodes, Reality Rent formulas) assume a world where AGI is already deployed and regulated, yet foundational questions about detection, measurement, and enforcement remain unaddressed.
- **Extreme Complexity:** The interlocking mechanisms (CINs, Reality Rent, Synthetic Ratio Mandate, multiple bond types, circuit breaker levels) create a governance architecture that would be extraordinarily difficult to implement even in stable conditions, let alone during the rapid technological upheaval this framework seeks to govern.
- **Assumes a GGF-Aligned World:** The framework presupposes universal adoption of the Treaty, functioning BAZs, and a cooperative corporate sector. It lacks robust transition mechanics for the 2026 reality where AGI development is concentrated in a few corporate labs operating outside any GGF framework.
- **Definitional Gaps:** Key concepts like "parasitic behavior," "capability thresholds," and the "Independent International Scientific Panel on AI" lack operational definitions. The framework asserts the need for these but does not provide them.

**Path to Improvement:** This framework needs to be split. One document should articulate the philosophical vision and long-term goals. A separate, living document should outline phased transition strategies that start from the current concentration of AGI development and build toward the commons vision incrementally, with far more attention to how leverage is created in the absence of universal adoption.
