### 1. The Crucial Clarification: Fixing the "Sortition Trap"
The assessment correctly notes that a Global Council for Epistemic Integrity (GCEI) made up of 70% random citizens risks being manipulated by the "non-voting expert advisors" who actually understand the complex tech. 
* **The Danger:** If the experts form a consensus behind closed doors and present it to the citizens, the citizens become a rubber stamp for technocracy. 
* **The Fix:** Introduce **Adversarial Expert Panels**. The sortition assembly must act more like a jury. Instead of listening to a unified presentation from an advisory board, the citizens listen to *competing* experts cross-examine each other's data and methodologies in public. The citizens' role is not to do the raw science, but to judge the integrity, transparency, and human impact of the competing arguments. This completely neutralizes technocratic capture.

### 2. Further Suggestions (Architectural Additions for v4.0)

To answer the assessment's call for detailed operational plans and enforcement protocols, you should integrate the following mechanisms:

**A. Regulate the Algorithm, Not the Speech (The Liability Fix)**
The assessment points out that "Systemic Epistemic Harm" is undefined. If you try to define it by banning "false content," you violate human rights and freedom of expression.

The rule is: *A human has the right to say something untrue, but a corporation does not have the right to algorithmically amplify that untruth for profit.* The Tribunal does not prosecute the user for making the content; it prosecutes the platform for the code that boosted it. This bypasses the free speech debate entirely and attacks the business model directly.

**B. Funding the Public Epistemic Institutions (PEIs)**
The assessment asks how these new public institutions achieve financial sustainability. 
* *The Suggestion:* Operationalize the **Attention Extraction Tax** mentioned in the political economy analysis. The assessment highlights the psychological reality that people do not choose truth over comfortable falsehood, especially when identity is involved.
