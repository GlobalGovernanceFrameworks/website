### 2. Answers to Your Questions

#### **Q1: Should Commons Trusts include non-human representatives (Legal Personhood)?**

**Answer: Yes. This is non-negotiable for a regenerative system.**

* **Why:** If the Lake is just "property," it has no voice. If it is a "Legal Person," it has standing.
* **How:** You don't put a fish in a chair. You use the **"Guardian Model"** (Te Awa Tupua style).
* *The Mechanism:* The Commons Trust seats for "Lake Mälaren" are filled by **Human Guardians** who have a fiduciary duty to the Lake, not the people.
* *Selection:* These Guardians should be a mix of **Indigenous Elders** (who carry the historical relationship) and **Ecologists** (who carry the data).
* *Power:* They vote purely on: *"Does this decision harm the Lake's ability to regenerate?"* If yes, they Veto.



#### **Q2: How do Indigenous governance systems integrate without forced fragmentation?**

**Answer: Do NOT force them into one chamber. Make them the "Root."**

* **The Problem:** You are right. Indigenous cosmology often sees Economy, Governance, and Nature as one ("The Hoop"). Splitting them into three chambers feels colonial.
* **The Solution: The "Sovereignty Layer" (The Root).**
* Instead of making Indigenous nations a "Guild" or a "voting bloc" in the Assembly, recognize them as the **Host**.
* **The Mechanism:** The Indigenous Council acts as the **Constitutional Court** or **"Wisdom Council."** They don't vote on zoning laws (Assembly work). They hold the **Veto Power** over the *structure itself*.
* *Example:* If the Tri-Cameral system proposes a mining project that violates the **Great Law** or **Natural Law**, the Indigenous Council strikes it down. They are the "judges of the protocol," not the "players of the game."



#### **Q3: Urban applications where ecological boundaries are less obvious?**

**Answer: The City is a "Metabolic Node," not an Island.**

* **The Problem:** A city like Stockholm doesn't have a "forest" inside it to govern. Its footprint is 100x its physical size.
* **The Solution: "Shed-Federation."**
* The Urban BAZ (e.g., Stockholm) cannot exist alone. It must be **Federated** with the Rural BAZs that feed it.
* *The Commons Trust in the City:* Does not just manage the city parks. It manages the **Metabolic Flow** (Water in, Waste out).
* *The Mechanism:* The City BAZ pays **Leaves** (External Currency) to the Upstream Rural BAZ for "Ecosystem Services" (Clean Water).
* *The Check:* If the City pollutes the outflow, the Downstream BAZ sues them in the **Bioregional Commons Trust**.


* **Reframing:** Don't look for "Ecological Boundaries" *inside* the city (like a park fence). Look for the **Pipe**. The boundary of an urban BAZ is its **Sewershed** and **Watershed**.
