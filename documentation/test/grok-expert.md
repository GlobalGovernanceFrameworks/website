**Wisdom Hearth Protocol**  
**Draft Outline for the Digital Commons Framework** (Tier 3, Cultural & Knowledge Commons)

### 1. Ecosystem Integration (proposed positioning)

This framework sits squarely inside the **Cultural & Knowledge Commons** cluster at Tier 3. It is **not** a second data-governance layer; Aurora Accord already owns personal/community/public data sovereignty, fiduciary standards, taxation, and the Global Digital Governance Council apparatus. The Wisdom Hearth Protocol instead treats **cultural knowledge, stories, artistic expressions, adapted Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK), and collective wisdom** as living gifts that move through the commons rather than as data assets to be stewarded as property.

**Dependencies it explicitly leans on**  
- **Aurora Accord (Tier 1)**: borrows the three-fold data classification and BCI Consent Protocols only for the technical consent layer on uploads; does not duplicate fiduciary certification or Digital Justice Tribunal enforcement.  
- **Education Framework (Tier 2)**: depends on it for baseline digital-literacy pathways so that Local Citizen Nodes are actually accessible rather than performative.  
- **Indigenous & Traditional Knowledge Framework**: supplies the guardianship model and Earth Council veto pathways for TEK-derived knowledge.  
- **Hearthstone Protocol (Property & Stewardship)**: inherits the ownership-to-stewardship transition language for intangible/IP assets so that “knowledge gifts” are never re-commodified.  
- **Nested Economies Framework**: routes informal knowledge contributions through the existing **Love Ledger** + **Proof of Care** scoring (with a new “cultural resonance multiplier” 0.8–1.3× tied to LMCI principles).  
- **Synoptic Protocol**: supplies the epistemic-integrity checksum before any cross-BAZ sharing.

**What it enables downstream**  
- Supplies the living archive layer for **Cultural Heritage Preservation**.  
- Provides the shared substrate for **Religious & Spiritual Dialogue** and **Sacred Data Protocol** integration (sacred knowledge lives here under guardian gating).  
- Hosts the **Wisdom Repository** that Inner Development & Leadership Protocol and Consciousness Development draw from.  
- Feeds **Arts Flourishing** with open-access creative commons material that is provenance-tracked but not monetized.

**Governance entities it establishes**  
- **Digital Commons Council (DCC)** – Tier 3 coordination body.  
- **Local Citizen Nodes (LCNs)** – one per BAZ, fractal and physical-first.

No new global taxation, no new tribunal, no new encryption stack — it rides on Aurora’s Minimum Viable Security and Nested Economies’ translation layer.

### 2. Core Governance Mechanisms (operational detail only)

**Mechanism 1 – Digital Commons Council (DCC)**  
Composition (fixed, not negotiable at launch):  
- 40 % delegates elected from LCNs (one per 10 BAZs, fractal recall possible).  
- 40 % Indigenous & marginalized representatives (nominated via Earth Council liaison, 50 %+ veto quorum on TEK-related items).  
- 20 % rotating “enabling cluster” seats (Arts, Consciousness Development, Education).  
Meets hybrid every 90 days. Proposals originate only from LCNs and must carry a Proof of Care score ≥ 3.0. Decision rule: spiral-aware consent (not majority) — every participant states their developmental-stage objection; unresolved objections trigger a 21-day MGCC mediation. Output: binding interoperability standards and annual “Resonance Report” on knowledge health. DCC has no enforcement power; it flags violations to Digital Justice Tribunal or Earth Council.

**Mechanism 2 – Local Citizen Nodes (LCNs)**  
One physical-digital hub per BAZ (can be a community hall with offline-first tablet + solar uplink).  
Operational flow for a knowledge gift:  
1. Contributor uploads via Aurora-derived consent interface (personal/community/sacred tags).  
2. Local Lore Weaver (6-month trained role, paid Layer 2 AUBI) performs 72-hour fiduciary review against MOS concentric rings (does this gift harm ecosystems/sentient beings/future beings?).  
3. Scored with existing Proof of Care × cultural-resonance multiplier; auto-recorded in Love Ledger.  
4. Local copy stays immutable in the node; global mirror to Wisdom Repository only after explicit “gift consent” flag and 14-day BAZ objection window.  
5. Contributor can revoke consent at any time (Right to Exit mirrored from Aurora); the gift is then soft-deleted from public view but retained in provenance log for accountability.

**Mechanism 3 – Lore Flow Protocol (cross-BAZ sharing process)**  
Quarterly “Flow Windows” open for 30 days. Any LCN can propose a bundle of knowledge for wider circulation. Bundle receives Synoptic Protocol integrity hash + DCC pre-check. Receiving BAZs vote via their own citizen assemblies (simple 60 % + no Earth Council veto). Accepted bundles appear in the recipient node with full provenance chain and attribution. Rejected bundles stay local; repeated rejections trigger DCC-facilitated dialogue, not compulsion. The protocol logs every hop in the Love Ledger so that “knowledge caregiving” is visible and can be rewarded with Hearts via the Nested Economies inter-currency layer.

### 3. Genuine Tension It Must Hold Structurally

**Core paradox**: Radical openness (required for arts flourishing, spiritual dialogue, consciousness development, and evolutionary learning) versus protective stewardship (required to prevent cultural extraction, appropriation, and epistemic erosion — especially of Indigenous and sacred knowledge).

The framework does not “resolve” this with a values statement. It embeds the tension in three structural features that force ongoing negotiation:  
- **Layered visibility** inherited from MOS concentric rings: public “open hearth” layer (anyone can read/annotate), guardian-gated “inner hearth” layer (consent + fiduciary review required), and sovereign “sealed hearth” layer (Indigenous veto + zero external access). Movement between layers requires explicit DCC + Earth Council sign-off.  
- **Immutable core + living annotation branches**: original cultural artifacts are frozen (preservation), but communities can attach “evolution forks” with full provenance. Forks can be merged only after a 90-day Slow-Variable Constraint window (mirroring Nested Economies) and only if the originating community consents. This keeps the archive alive without erasing origins.  
- **Mandatory sovereignty audits** every 18 months: DCC publishes a public report showing % of knowledge that moved from sealed → open vs. open → sealed. Any BAZ or Indigenous representative can trigger an immediate Earth Council review if the ratio drifts >15 % in either direction. The audit itself is the mechanism — it does not dictate an ideal ratio.

The tension is therefore visible, measurable, and renegotiable at every scale. It cannot be wished away by good intentions.

### 4. Naming and Terminology (GGF voice)

**Official name**: Wisdom Hearth Protocol  
**Short name**: Wisdom Hearth  

Key terms chosen for productive friction:  
- **Lore Gift** – a contribution that is given, never sold.  
- **Lore Weaver** – the trained local role (parallels Community Weaver).  
- **Hearth Resonance** – the cultural-impact multiplier in Proof of Care scoring.  
- **Living Archive** – the Wisdom Repository (explicitly not “database”).  
- **Sealed / Inner / Open Hearth** – the three visibility layers.  

The name deliberately echoes Hearthstone Protocol while staying distinct: the hearth is the fire itself, not the stone. It is slightly too honest — “wisdom” is not neutral; “hearth” evokes care, vulnerability, and shared vulnerability in a way institutional language avoids.

### 5. Honest Uncertainty and Epistemic Status

This is a draft outline only.  

I am uncertain about:  
- Exact hand-off points with the (unseen) Education Framework and full Indigenous Framework; the dependency arrows I drew are inferred from the ecosystem map and may need recalibration.  
- Technical feasibility of offline-first LCNs at true planetary scale without creating new central chokepoints (Aurora’s security stack helps, but real-world resilience testing is required).  
- How the Sacred Data Protocol expects to “integrate” — my assumption of guardian-gated layers may be too coarse or too fine.  
- Whether the cultural-resonance multiplier creates unintended gaming incentives similar to the metric failures MOS warns against.  

Expert input needed from: Earth Council representatives, existing Lore Weavers or cultural stewards already operating inside BAZs, and the Global Technology Council on decentralized storage costs.  

The outline proposes these mechanisms and relationships; it does not claim they are proven or ready for deployment. Validation through MGCC stress-testing and a 12-month pilot in 5–10 voluntary BAZs would be the next required step.  

Ready for feedback, especially where the tension-holding mechanisms feel either too tight or too loose.
