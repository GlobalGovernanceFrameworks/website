## 8. Success Indicators (Qualitative, Community-Owned)

Following the Memorial Commons model, we track **care indicators**, not algorithmic KPIs.

| **Indicator** | **Question** | **Method** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ritual Vitality** | Are communities creating and sustaining their own rituals? | Annual narrative reports; self-documentation |
| **Narrative Resilience** | Are regenerative stories circulating and countering nihilistic/weaponized narratives? | Storybank growth; community perception surveys; narrative analysis workshops |
| **Existential Capacity** | Do people report feeling more equipped to navigate mortality, uncertainty, and purpose? | Qualitative interviews; reflection circles |
| **Grief Integration** | Is collective grief being processed rather than suppressed or weaponized? | Memorial Commons care indicators; community observation |
| **Protected Spaces Integrity** | Are designated Protected Meaning Spaces remaining free from optimization pressure and encroachment? | Annual review by Meaning Stewardship Circle; community testimony |
