## 6. A Center of Gravity: The "Living Story" Anchor

A purely distributed toolkit risks feeling fragmented. Humans orient around shared reference points—myths, archetypes, evolving narratives.

This protocol proposes a **symbolic anchor**, not a centralized institution:

> **The Living Story of the Bioregion:** Each BAZ is invited to steward a *living narrative*—an evolving, pluralistic account of its place: its ecological history, its cultural lineages, its ongoing journey of regeneration. This is not a single official story but a *container* for multiple stories, held by the Narrative Stewardship Guild and expressed through ritual, art, and memorial.

This anchor provides a **center of gravity** without centralizing power. It answers the question: *"What story are we part of?"*—not with a fixed answer, but with an ongoing, participatory inquiry.
