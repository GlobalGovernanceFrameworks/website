## 1. Executive Summary

The Sundown Protocol provides a just, compassionate, and enforceable pathway for retiring industries that are incompatible with planetary health — fossil fuels, extractive mining, industrial agriculture, and others identified by the Planetary Health Council.

### The Challenge

Legacy industries represent trillions in infrastructure and employ millions. They cannot be left to chaotic market-driven decline, which would devastate communities and strand workers. Yet voluntary transition is insufficient to meet planetary boundary timelines. We need a managed, just, and binding process.

### The Opportunity

This protocol is not anti-industry. It is pro-future. It offers a roadmap for vital sectors and their workforces to "graduate" into the regenerative economy — protecting workers, healing ecosystems, and positioning first-movers as global leaders in the new economy.

### How It Works

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Trigger** | The Planetary Health Council identifies industries for sunset based on Biosphere Health Index thresholds |
| **Standards** | The Global Transition Standards Board sets just transition requirements |
| **Execution** | BAZ Transition Authorities manage local dismantling, retraining, and remediation |
| **Protection** | Workers receive AUBI income replacement and retraining through the Capacity Engine |
| **Accountability** | Corporations post Pre-Emptive Ecological Bonds to prevent bankruptcy loopholes |

## 2. Core Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Just Transition First** | The well-being, security, and future prosperity of workers and communities are the primary success metrics |
| **Polluter Pays & Precautionary Principle** | Legacy industries primarily fund the transition; the burden does not fall on the public |
| **Pre-Emptive Accountability** | Corporations post ecological bonds before sunset begins, preventing bankruptcy avoidance |
| **Subsidiarity** | Global standards, local execution. BAZs and local unions manage the transition; global bodies set requirements |
| **Indigenous Sovereignty** | Transition and remediation on traditional lands require FPIC 2.0; Indigenous monitoring teams have halt authority |
| **Orderly Decommissioning** | Managed to prevent economic shocks, ensuring energy and resource stability |
| **Regenerative Advantage** | First-movers gain expertise in decommissioning and restoration, becoming global leaders |
| **Enforceable Compliance** | Non-compliance triggers procurement embargos, asset freezes, and market exclusion |

## 3. Institutional Architecture

The Sundown Protocol uses three bodies with clear roles. This replaces the complex web of councils, offices, and teams in previous versions.

### 3.1 Three Bodies, Clear Hierarchy

| Body | Role | Composition |
|------|------|-------------|
| **Planetary Health Council (existing)** | Sets the trigger: which industries, by what deadline, based on Biosphere Health Index (BHI) thresholds | Existing GGF body |
| **Global Transition Standards Board** | Sets standards: what constitutes a just transition, remediation requirements, certification criteria, and bond calculations | 50% workers/unions, 25% Indigenous representatives, 25% technical experts |
| **BAZ Transition Authorities** | Executes locally: manages dismantling, retraining, remediation, and asset transfer within BAZ boundaries | BAZ councils + local unions + Indigenous governments + affected community representatives |

### 3.2 What Was Eliminated

| Eliminated Body | Rationale |
|-----------------|-----------|
| Global Transition Council | Redundant; Planetary Health Council already exists |
| Office of Just Transition | Functions absorbed by BAZ Transition Authorities |
| Just Transition SWAT | Crisis response handled by BAZs with regional coordination |
| Transition Mediation Corps | Mediation handled by existing Peace & Conflict Resolution Framework |
| Legacy Assurance Unit | Audit function absorbed by BAZ oversight + Indigenous monitoring |
| Community Transition Board | Absorbed into BAZ Transition Authority composition |

### 3.3 What Was Retained (Simplified)

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| **Just Transition Fund** | Manages bonds, disburses funds for remediation and worker support |
| **Indigenous Monitoring Teams** | Independent oversight; halt authority for FPIC violations |
| **Transition Heritage Council** | Cultural body guiding narrative, rituals, and heritage preservation |

### 3.4 Indigenous Monitoring Teams

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Authority** | Independent oversight teams appointed by Indigenous governing bodies |
| **Powers** | Halt any activity violating FPIC 2.0; enforce Sacred Site Remediation Guidelines; maintain Red Lines Registry |
| **Funding** | Directly from Just Transition Fund, with full independence from corporate or BAZ control |
| **Accountability** | Report to Planetary Health Council; decisions appealable only to Digital Justice Tribunal |

## 4. Pre-Ratification Strategy: Building Leverage

The Sundown Protocol does not assume the Treaty for Our Only Home is fully ratified or that the Digital Justice Tribunal has binding authority. This section provides mechanisms for building leverage before those institutions exist.

### 4.1 Pathfinder Compact

Voluntary coalition of BAZs, cities, and nations committed to the transition.

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Commitment** | Signatories commit to sunset target industries by agreed timelines |
| **Benefits** | Preferential access to Global Commons Fund support; technical assistance; leadership recognition |
| **Coordination** | Pathfinder members coordinate procurement, investment, and trade policies |

### 4.2 Procurement Embargo

The primary enforcement mechanism before global treaties exist.

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Commitment** | Pathfinder members legally bind themselves to refuse all public contracts, subsidies, and market access to corporations in target industries that refuse to enter the sunset process |
| **Scope** | Includes municipal, regional, and national procurement; infrastructure projects; public services |
| **Implementation** | Model legislation provided; coordinated through Pathfinder network |
| **Consequence** | Non-compliant corporations lose access to markets representing trillions in annual procurement |

### 4.3 Supply Chain Exclusion

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Requirement** | Pathfinder members require all suppliers to certify they are not sourcing from non-compliant corporations |
| **Verification** | Digital Product Passports (Gaian Trade Framework) track supply chain compliance |
| **Escalation** | Non-compliant suppliers are excluded from Pathfinder supply chains |

### 4.4 Divestment Coordination

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Commitment** | Pathfinder pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, and public investment vehicles commit to divesting from non-compliant corporations |
| **Coordination** | Shared divestment timelines; coordinated public announcements |
| **Target** | Create capital markets penalty for non-compliance |

### 4.5 Escalation Ladder

| Level | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **1. Public Naming** | Non-compliant corporations listed on Public Transition Dashboard |
| **2. Contract Exclusion** | Pathfinder members exclude from public contracts |
| **3. Supply Chain Exclusion** | Pathfinder supply chains reject non-compliant sources |
| **4. Investment Freeze** | Pathfinder public funds divest; new investment prohibited |
| **5. Asset Freeze** | Assets in Pathfinder jurisdictions frozen pending compliance |

## 5. Implementation Pathway

### 5.1 Simplified Timeline

| Phase | Duration | Key Activities | Decision Point |
|-------|----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Phase 0: Pathfinder** | Ongoing | Voluntary coalition building; procurement embargos; pilot transitions | Pathfinder Compact signed |
| **Phase 1: Designation** | Year 1 | Planetary Health Council triggers sunset; bond posted; timeline set | Sunset mandate issued |
| **Phase 2: Roadmap** | Years 1-2 | BAZ Transition Authorities co-create plan with unions, communities, Indigenous governments | Roadmap ratified locally |
| **Phase 3: Transition** | Years 3-15+ | Managed decommissioning; worker retraining; remediation; asset transfer | BHI improvement verified |
| **Phase 4: Stewardship** | Ongoing | Remediated land under BAZ stewardship; long-term monitoring | Transfer completed |

### 5.2 Adaptive Mechanisms

| Factor | Adjustment |
|--------|------------|
| **Technology Breakthrough** | Timeline accelerates if technology enables faster transition |
| **Worker Transition Needs** | Timeline extends if worker transition requires more time |
| **Climate Urgency** | Planetary Health Council can accelerate timeline based on BHI thresholds |
| **Crisis** | Crisis Command Protocol may temporarily pause or accelerate based on emergency |

### 5.3 Phase Details

**Phase 0: Pathfinder (Ongoing)**
- Voluntary coalition building
- Procurement embargos established
- Pilot transitions in willing jurisdictions
- Pathfinder Compact signed

**Phase 1: Designation (Year 1)**
- Planetary Health Council issues binding sunset trigger based on BHI thresholds
- Target corporations notified
- Pre-Emptive Ecological Bond posted (see Section 7.2)
- Sunset timeline set (e.g., 10-15 years depending on industry)

**Phase 2: Roadmap (Years 1-2)**
- BAZ Transition Authority convenes unions, communities, Indigenous governments
- Binding Transition Roadmap developed, including:
  - Decommissioning schedule
  - Worker retraining plan (tethered to Capacity Engine)
  - Community investment priorities
  - Remediation plan
  - Asset transfer plan
- Roadmap ratified locally; Global Transition Standards Board certifies compliance

**Phase 3: Transition (Years 3-15+)**
- Managed decommissioning proceeds per roadmap
- Workers retrained and placed in Community Work Teams
- Just Transition Fund disburses support
- Indigenous Monitoring Teams oversee remediation
- Remediated land/assets prepared for transfer

**Phase 4: Stewardship (Ongoing)**
- Remediated land/assets transferred to BAZ Stewardship Trusts
- Long-term monitoring (10-year intervals) ensures ecological recovery
- Youth Future Councils hold veto power over long-term land use plans

## 6. Worker & Community Protection

Worker protections are tethered to existing GGF frameworks rather than inventing new mechanisms.

### 6.1 Income Protection

| Element | Mechanism |
|---------|-----------|
| **Income Replacement** | Workers displaced by sunset mandates are automatically enrolled in AUBI at the highest tier |
| **Duration** | Full income replacement for 3 years, tapering to 70% until re-employment |
| **No Gap** | Enrollment begins immediately upon displacement; no waiting period |

### 6.2 Retraining & New Employment

| Element | Mechanism |
|---------|-----------|
| **Retraining** | Workers access the Capacity Engine for retraining; certifications recognized across BAZs |
| **Pathways** | Priority training in remediation, renewable energy, regenerative agriculture, and community care |
| **Employment** | Retrained workers placed in Community Work Teams (Work in Liberation Framework) for remediation work |
| **Placement Target** | 90% of displaced workers re-employed within 24 months |

### 6.3 Healthcare & Housing

| Element | Mechanism |
|---------|-----------|
| **Toxic Exposure** | Lifetime healthcare coverage for pollution-linked illnesses funded by Just Transition Fund |
| **Relocation** | Subsidies for workers who must relocate; priority access to Community Land Trust housing (Hearthstone Protocol) |
| **Mental Health** | Counseling and psychological support through Mental Health Governance Framework |

### 6.4 Community Reparations

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Health Legacy Monitoring** | Independent monitoring of pollution-affected communities; ongoing healthcare funding |
| **Infrastructure Investment** | Priority funding for community infrastructure in affected areas (schools, clinics, transit) |
| **Economic Diversification** | Just Transition Fund allocates capital for new economic activity in transitioning regions |

### 6.5 The Transition Workforce Bill of Rights

Every worker affected by a sunset mandate is guaranteed:

1. **Income Security:** AUBI income replacement from day one
2. **Retraining:** Free access to Capacity Engine retraining programs
3. **New Employment:** Placement in Community Work Teams
4. **Healthcare:** Lifetime coverage for toxic exposure
5. **Housing:** Relocation support and housing access
6. **Dignity:** Ceremonial recognition of service
7. **Voice:** Representation on BAZ Transition Authority

## 7. Financial Mechanisms

### 7.1 Just Transition Fund

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Purpose** | Manages bonds, disburses funds for remediation and worker support |
| **Oversight** | Co-chaired by Global Transition Standards Board and Indigenous Monitoring Teams |
| **Transparency** | Real-time dashboard on public transition platform; audited annually |

### 7.2 Pre-Emptive Ecological Bond

This closes the bankruptcy loophole that has allowed extractive industries to abandon cleanup costs.

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Trigger** | When an industry is designated for sunset under Phase 1, all corporations operating in that sector must post a financial bond within 12 months |
| **Bond Size** | 100% of estimated ecological cleanup cost, calculated by independent assessors (BAZ environmental councils + Indigenous monitoring teams) |
| **Bond Structure** | Held in escrow by Just Transition Fund. Cannot be used as collateral. Cannot be insured against. Executive bonuses frozen until bond is posted. |
| **Consequence of Non-Compliance** | If bond not posted, the corporation's physical assets (mines, wells, facilities) are immediately placed under BAZ trusteeship. Intellectual property licenses for transition-relevant technologies are made openly available under compulsory license. |

**Why This Matters:** Without this mechanism, corporations extract value until the last moment, declare bankruptcy, and leave cleanup to taxpayers. The bond ensures they cannot escape accountability.

### 7.3 Funding Sources

| Source | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pre-Emptive Ecological Bonds** | Primary funding for remediation |
| **Reparations Levy** | Additional levy on historical emissions and extraction |
| **Transition Bonds** | Issued by Pathfinder members, backed by Global Commons Fund guarantees |
| **Global Commons Fund** | Base funding for worker support and capacity building |

### 7.4 Reparations Levy

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Base** | Calculated on historical emissions and extraction (1990-present) |
| **Rate** | Set by Global Transition Standards Board, calibrated to avoid economic shock |
| **Allocation** | 60% to Just Transition Fund, 20% to worker support, 20% to Global South capacity building |

## 9. Cultural & Ceremonial Transition

Industrial sunset is not just economic; it is cultural, psychological, and spiritual. This section honors that dimension without letting it overwhelm practical mechanisms.

### 9.1 Transition Heritage Council

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Composition** | Workers, community members, Indigenous knowledge holders, youth, artists |
| **Mandate** | Guides narrative, preserves industrial knowledge, co-designs closure rituals |
| **Funding** | Direct from Just Transition Fund (2% of annual disbursement) |

### 9.2 Closure Ceremonies

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Purpose** | Community-led rituals marking the end of extraction — not as failure, but as honorable evolution |
| **Design** | Co-designed with workers, Indigenous knowledge holders, and community members |
| **Examples** | "Retiring the Last Coal Mine," "Healing the Mine," "The Last Rig" |
| **Funding** | Supported by Just Transition Fund |

### 9.3 Heritage Preservation

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Oral Histories** | Worker narratives preserved in Global Transition Story Archive |
| **Industrial Archaeology** | Significant sites preserved as educational/cultural centers |
| **Knowledge Transfer** | Technical and operational knowledge documented for future generations |
| **Intergenerational Storytelling** | Circles connecting elder workers with youth |

### 9.4 Narrative Framing

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **The Message** | Transition is not defeat; it is graduation. "The Last Coal Miner, The First Geothermal Engineer." |
| **Amplification** | Transition Ambassadors program trains workers and community leaders to share their stories globally |
| **Platform** | Global Transition Story Archive hosted on Digital Commons |
