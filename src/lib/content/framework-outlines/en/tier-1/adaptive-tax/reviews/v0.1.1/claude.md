**Three residual items, none blocking**

*A third orphan of the same family as §20.5.* GSCL line 118 funds regional hubs "via **FSF's** Resource & Environmental Taxes and Financial Transaction Taxes." Financial Systems v3.4's scope section says it does not replace tax law. So GSCL attributes two instruments to a framework that disclaims them — same failure mode as Digital Commons routing the Automation Dividend and Data Exploitation taxes to Aurora. §20.4 lists these as GSCL "funding assumptions," which slightly misdescribes the defect: the instruments themselves are legitimate Treaty Art 18.2 classes (financial-transaction levies, extractive/commons charges), so the correction is repointing the attribution, not migrating a proposal. Worth a distinct line, since the disposition differs.

*The Symmetrical Imbalance Penalty deserves naming.* Gaian Trade line 24 taxes nations running large trade surpluses and directs the revenue to the GCF. §20.4 catches it under "imbalance-penalty revenue rules," but §18.12's body doesn't. It's the single most constitutionally unusual fiscal instrument in the corpus — an inter-state charge on trade position, which is neither a Party contribution under Art 18.3 nor any enumerated class under 18.2. Given §1.5 already does the careful work of showing personal wealth taxation isn't in Art 18, this one needs the same treatment rather than sitting in a migration bullet.

*The framework only prohibits automation in one direction.* §9.6(4) and the new §22 gate both target indicator → punitive tariff. But Gaian Trade line 98's Food Sovereignty Circuit Breaker automatically *suspends* staple-goods tariffs when food-access indicators cross emergency thresholds, explicitly "not through slow political negotiation." That's indicator → relief, and CIS §11.1 treats protective reversible automation differently from consequential automation. As drafted, Adaptive Tax's rule is silent on it, which either implies it's fine without analysis or implies it's caught by a rule aimed at something else. One sentence stating the asymmetry — automatic relief within an enacted schedule is permissible under §2.1's formulaic-adaptation bounds, automatic penalty is not — would close it, and it happens to be a finding in Gaian Trade's favour.

I'd fold those three in and call v0.1.2 ready to move to the schema wiring.
