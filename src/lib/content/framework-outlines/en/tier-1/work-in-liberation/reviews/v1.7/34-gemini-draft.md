### 2.5. The end of the job application: biomimetic project assembly

Under the traditional employment model, the job application process functions as a lossy compression of human potential. It forces multidimensional individuals into static, one-dimensional "job descriptions" managed by centralized human resources departments. Work in Liberation dismantles this high-friction bottleneck, replacing it with a biomimetic model of fluid project assembly modeled on how living ecosystems coordinate resources.

Because AUBI Layer 1 secures baseline survival, individuals no longer need to lock themselves into rigid 40-hour compliance containers. Work becomes a dynamic flow of contribution rather than a static identity.

**Key mechanisms of biomimetic assembly:**

* **Need broadcasting over job postings:** Instead of writing rigid job descriptions for permanent roles, Community Providers and BAZ councils broadcast specific "needs" or "problems to be solved" to the local network.
* **Capability-sensing via Mycel:** The matching process shifts from credential-matching to capability-sensing. Utilizing the Mycel ambient network—a decentralized, trust-weighted mesh—local needs organically find individuals with the corresponding capacities and intrinsic motivations. Mycel acts as the digital mycelium, surfacing relevant humans without requiring them to constantly self-promote or optimize resumes.
* **Project fluidity and the 15-hour cap:** People dynamically assemble around projects based on their self-scheduled 15-hour Flexible Time cap. A community member might flow seamlessly between projects, dedicating 5 hours to a watershed restoration team and 10 hours to an eldercare provider in the same week. When a specific project is resolved, the temporary assembly dissolves, but the underlying network of relationships remains permanent.
* **Reputation as a living trace:** The need for external validation (references and background checks) is replaced by visible contribution. The Love Ledger and Hearts transaction histories leave a privacy-preserving but highly visible trace of collaboration, reliability, and skill. Trust is established through a continuous record of actual work, not through the performative ritual of an interview.

By removing the administrative waste of the job-seeking industrial complex, this biomimetic approach allows work to become what a person *does*—in natural alignment with their shifting capacities—rather than who a person *is*.
