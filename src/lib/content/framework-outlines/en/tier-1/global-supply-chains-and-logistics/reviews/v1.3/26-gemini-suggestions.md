Here are four structural, systems-level suggestions that have not been addressed by either model:

### 1. The Jevons Paradox Firewall (Thermodynamic Accounting)
**The Gap:** Pillar 2.1 focuses on making supply chains more efficient and reducing emissions. However, in complex systems, the **Jevons Paradox** dictates that increasing the efficiency of a resource/network usually *lowers* its cost, which paradoxically *increases* total aggregate consumption. If GSCL makes shipping 30% more efficient, the market will simply use that saved capacity to ship 30% more cheap, unnecessary goods.
**The Solution:** Introduce an **"Efficiency Rebound Cap"** under Pillar 2.1 or 3.
* **Mechanism:** Legally mandate that whenever a bioregional supply chain achieves a measurable efficiency gain (e.g., a 10% reduction in transport energy), a portion of that saved capacity is structurally retired or placed into the "Planetary Reserve," rather than being absorbed as expanded commercial throughput. 
* **Metric:** Shift from just measuring "CO2 reduction" to measuring **EROTI (Energy Return on Transported Investment)**. The system must evaluate if the caloric/energy cost of moving an item is justified by its societal utility.

### 2. Algorithmic Pluralism (Preventing AI Monoculture Gridlock)
**The Gap:** Pillar 2.3 relies heavily on AI to predict disruptions and reroute supplies (e.g., "AI reroutes supplies via inland BAZ hubs within 36 hours"). If global or regional logistics rely on the same foundational AI models or optimized routing logic, you create a **Cognitive Monoculture**. During a crisis, every AI will independently calculate the "next best route" and send *all* traffic to that exact same route simultaneously, causing a catastrophic secondary bottleneck (a logistics "flash crash").
**The Solution:** Mandate **"Algorithmic Pluralism"** within the Aurora Accord / Pillar 2.2.
* **Mechanism:** BAZs are required to use heterogeneous, locally trained routing algorithms rather than relying on a single global predictive model. 
* **Why it matters:** Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety applies to AI too. You need diverse routing logic to ensure traffic diffuses organically through the mesh network during a crisis rather than swarming the single mathematically optimal backup route.

### 3. The "Empty Mile" Reverse-Logistics Mandate
**The Gap:** The framework mentions "circular economies," but modern logistics is architected almost entirely for *forward* movement (factory to consumer). It is terribly inefficient at *reverse* movement (bringing materials back to the start). Up to 30% of global trucking miles are driven completely empty after a drop-off.
**The Solution:** Add an **"Anastomotic Reverse-Flow Protocol"** to Pillar 2.1.
* **Mechanism:** It becomes a violation of the GSCL framework to run "empty miles." Any vehicle or container returning from a forward delivery *must* be made available for reverse-logistics (carrying recyclables, composting materials, or repaired goods back to processing hubs). 
* **Incentive:** Subsidize these "reverse miles" heavily with *Hearts*. A truck driver moving e-waste back to a regional hub earns a massive *Leaves/Hearts* multiplier, turning waste recovery into a highly profitable logistical leg.

### 4. The "Dark Chain" Interception Protocol
**The Gap:** The framework relies on transparency (Digital Passports, Aurora Accord). However, when you impose high ethical/transparency standards, bad actors simply move into the shadows (e.g., "ghost fleets" moving sanctioned oil today). The framework needs a way to handle goods that intentionally lack data.
**The Solution:** Introduce **"Information Asymmetry Tariffs"** in Pillar 5 (Governance and Compliance).
* **Mechanism:** If a BTRH (Bioregional Trade and Resource Hub) receives goods or materials that have *no* digital provenance or broken data chains, the system doesn't just reject them—it automatically applies the maximum possible "Dark Chain Tariff." 
* **Why it matters:** You weaponize the absence of data. By making untraceable goods mathematically unprofitable to offload in any Regenerative Trade Zone, you starve the shadow economy of its off-ramps.
