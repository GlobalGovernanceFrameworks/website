## 2. The "Capture Early Warning System" – Prevent Elite Capture Before It Happens

## 3. The "Funding Asymmetry Problem" – Realism About Global South Capacity

You allocate 20% of funds to Global South infrastructure, which is admirable. But the *absorptive capacity* risk is real: many institutions lack grant-writing infrastructure, compliance staff, or open-access repositories.

**Proposed solution: The "Twinning Mandate"**

- Every Global North hub must twin with a Global South hub that receives:
  - 10% of the North hub's administrative budget for capacity building.
  - Shared credit for all publications (equal authorship by default).
  - Joint governance: North hub funding decisions require South hub co-signature.

**Why this matters:** Without active capacity transfer, equity funds become "unspent balances" returned to donors.
