## Final Polish: Edge-Case Armoring

### 1. **The "Data Poisoning by CIN" Vulnerability**

**The Threat:** A captured or malicious CIN could deliberately feed corrupted data into the training substrate to manipulate the Global Trunk's behavior (e.g., introducing subtle biases, backdoors, or propaganda).

**Current Defense:** Light-touch certification, governance charters, audits.

**Recommendation:** Add a **"Context Provenance Challenge" protocol:**
- All CIN-submitted data carries a cryptographic signature.
- Any BAZ or Trust member can flag suspicious data for a **randomized audit pool.**
- If corruption is confirmed, the CIN faces graduated sanctions: first offense = suspension and rent forfeiture; second = permanent revocation and public disclosure.
- Auditors themselves are subject to **cross-audit** by a different regional council to prevent audit capture.

---

### 2. **The "Emergent Capability Leakage" Problem**

**The Threat:** A local BAZ fine-tunes a model for benign purposes (e.g., agricultural optimization), but the fine-tuning process accidentally unlocks a dangerous capability that wasn't present in the Global Trunk (e.g., novel pathogen design emerges from crop genetics work).

**Current Defense:** Core safety invariants are non-negotiable at the branch level.

**Recommendation:** Add a **"Capability Mirroring" requirement:**
- Before any fine-tuned branch is deployed, it must pass through an automated **capability reflection scan** that compares its emergent behaviors against the Global Trunk's prohibited capability list.
- If the scan detects a prohibited capability, the branch is automatically sandboxed and the Arbitration Panel is notified within 24 hours.
- The scan itself is open-source and independently audited to prevent the Trust from using it to suppress legitimate innovation.

---

### 3. **The "Bond Evasion via Shell Entities" Risk**

**The Threat:** A corporation creates dozens of shell companies, each deploying small AI swarms. If one causes harm, the bond is forfeited, but the parent company's core assets remain untouched.

**Current Defense:** Bonds are non-transferable and non-financializable, but this doesn't address corporate structuring.

**Recommendation:** Add a **"Pierce the Corporate Veil" clause:**
- For bonding purposes, any entity that shares **beneficial ownership, control, or funding** with another deploying entity is considered a single economic unit.
- Bonds are calculated based on the **aggregate risk** of all entities under common control.
- If a shell company's bond is insufficient to cover damages, the parent company and all related entities are jointly and severally liable.

---

### 4. **The "Reality Rent Inflation" Tactic**

**The Threat:** CINs collude to artificially inflate the "Cultural Density/Vulnerability" factor in the rent formula, extracting excessive rents and making AGI development economically unviable for legitimate local applications.

**Current Defense:** Annual recalibration and third-party audits.

**Recommendation:** Add a **"Rent Reasonableness Review":**
- Any BAZ or deploying entity can challenge a CIN's rent calculation as "materially unreasonable" compared to similar CINs in comparable bioregions.
- The challenge goes to a **randomly selected panel of peer CINs** from different regions, ensuring local knowledge but preventing local capture.
- If the panel finds the rent excessive, the CIN must adjust and may face a temporary suspension of its ability to set exclusive rates.

---

### 5. **The "Zombie CIN" Problem**

**The Threat:** A CIN becomes inactive (members disperse, governance collapses) but continues to collect Reality Rent because no one initiates the dissolution process.

**Current Defense:** Not explicitly addressed.

**Recommendation:** Add a **"Use It or Lose It" clause:**
- Every CIN must file an **annual attestation of active governance**, including member rosters, meeting minutes, and context stewardship activities.
- If no attestation is filed for two consecutive years, the CIN is placed in "dormant status." Rent payments are held in escrow for one additional year.
- If no reactivation occurs, the CIN's context is released to the **Context Commons Reserve** (managed by the Trust) and rent is redirected to a global fund for training new CINs in underserved regions.

---

### 6. **The "Synthetic Data Threshold Gaming" Risk**

**The Threat:** A model developer keeps synthetic ratio just below the surcharge threshold (e.g., 39.9%) but uses synthetic data of such low quality that it still risks model collapse, just more slowly.

**Current Defense:** Threshold with multiplier.

**Recommendation:** Add a **"Synthetic Quality Index" (SQI) as a secondary factor:**
- Synthetic data is graded on diversity, novelty, and fidelity to real-world distributions (audited by a technical body).
- Even if below the 40% threshold, a low SQI triggers a **partial surcharge** (e.g., 1.5× instead of 2×).
- This prevents a "race to the bottom" in synthetic data quality.

## Structural Suggestion: Add a **Pillar VI - Grievance & Redress**

You have enforcement mechanisms (bonds, kill switches, audits) but no explicit channel for **individual humans** harmed by AI to seek redress. The current model assumes harm is to CINs or bioregions.

**Recommendation:** A short but crucial addition:

### **Pillar VI: Individual Grievance & Redress**

* **The Human-Scale Accountability Gap:** Acknowledging that systemic harms (CIN-level, bioregional) are covered by bonds, but individual harms (e.g., an AI denies a person a loan based on a hallucinated risk, or a medical AI misdiagnoses) need a direct path to remedy.
* **Digital Ombudsperson Network:** A federated network of human-staffed ombudsperson offices, accessible via simple interfaces (web, phone, community centers), where individuals can file grievances about AI decisions affecting them.
* **Grievance Triage:**
  - **Level 1 (Automated Mediation):** For clear errors (e.g., factual mistakes), the ombudsperson system contacts the deploying entity for expedited correction.
  - **Level 2 (Human Review):** For disputes involving rights, fairness, or dignity, a human investigator (trained in both AI systems and local context) conducts a review.
  - **Level 3 (Compensation):** If harm is confirmed, compensation is drawn from a **Global Redress Fund**, financed by a small percentage (e.g., 1%) of all Reality Rent and Risk Bond proceeds.
* **Appeals:** Individuals can appeal Level 2 decisions to the regional Arbitration Panel.
* **No AI in the Loop for Grievance:** All grievance adjudication involving human dignity must ultimately be decided by humans, not AI.

This closes the loop between macro-governance and micro-impact. Without it, the Accord governs systems but not their effect on actual people.
