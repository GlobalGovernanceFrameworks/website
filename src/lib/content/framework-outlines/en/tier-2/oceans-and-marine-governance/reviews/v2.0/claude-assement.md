"Awaken the Consciousness" is worse; it maps poorly onto what's actually happening in Phase 3 (legal personhood scaling, shipping decarbonization). **2. Governance Complexity / Relationship to Existing Bodies — Substantially addressed.**
This is where v2.0 has improved most. Section 3.1 now explicitly states the MGCC "does not replace RFMOs; sets standards that BAZs adopt." Section 3.2 clarifies BAZs operate within national EEZs. Section 3.4 states the Data Commons Trust "interfaces with existing monitoring programs; provides open data to RFMOs and national authorities." The GDGC from DeepSeek's list has been replaced by the cleaner "Global Data Commons Trust." The Blue Heart Cultural Council is now explicitly positioned as advisory ("non-operational, but recommendations carry significant weight"). This is much clearer than before.

One remaining issue: the MGCC composition gives states only 10% representation. For a body that "sets global standards" and interfaces with UNCLOS and the ISA, this is a political non-starter. States won't accept binding standards from a body where they have 10% voice. This isn't about whether Indigenous-led governance is desirable — it's about whether this composition would ever achieve buy-in from the nations whose EEZs and port authorities make enforcement possible. You might consider a dual-chamber model, or frame the MGCC explicitly as a GGF-internal coordination body rather than something that "sets global standards" unilaterally.
