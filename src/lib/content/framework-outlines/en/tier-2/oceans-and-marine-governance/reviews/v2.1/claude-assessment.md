What happens when the Stewardship Assembly proposes something the State Assembly blocks? There's no deadlock resolution mechanism. A note on what happens after a failed vote — perhaps resubmission with amendments, or advisory-only status — would close this gap.

**Pillar III's biotech ethics framework is promising but has no named governance body.** The three-stage review process exists, but who conducts stages 1 and 2? Stage 3 is the Blue Heart Cultural Council, but the technical and ecological assessments need a responsible body — likely the MGCC's Stewardship Assembly or a dedicated scientific panel.

Given that banning bottom trawling by 2030 will displace hundreds of thousands of workers, this needs at least a brief structural mechanism — similar to the Capacity Engine's worker transition or the Phoenix Protocol's EPR fund allocation.

The KPIs reference "50% plastic reduction" in Phase 2, but the core mechanisms don't include any plastic-specific intervention. A brief note connecting to the Phoenix Protocol's Extended Producer Responsibility for plastic packaging, or a marine-specific mechanism, would close this.
