**We don’t have an information problem.
We have a reality problem.**

Right now, the most powerful systems on Earth are not designed to show us what’s true —
they’re designed to show us what keeps us hooked.

Outrage spreads faster than accuracy.
Fear outperforms nuance.
And the result is something we’re only beginning to understand:

We are losing our shared reality.

Not because people are stupid.
Not because truth disappeared.
But because the *system rewards distortion*.

---

**So what if we changed the system?**

Not by banning speech.
Not by deciding what people are allowed to say.

But by changing one thing:

👉 **What gets amplified.**

---

Imagine this:

• You can say anything — but platforms are responsible for what they push to millions
• Public-interest media is funded like infrastructure — not driven by clicks
• People are taught how to think, not what to think
• Disagreements don’t escalate into tribes — but into real conversations
• And in a crisis, truth spreads faster than panic

---

This isn’t about controlling reality.

It’s about **protecting the conditions that make reality discoverable at all.**

---

Call it a simple principle:

👉 **Freedom of speech is sacred.
Freedom of reach is not.**

---

We’ve spent 20 years optimizing for attention.

Maybe it’s time to start optimizing for understanding.

Because without a shared reality,
we can’t solve anything else.

Not climate.
Not pandemics.
Not democracy.

Nothing.

---

**The question isn’t “Who decides what’s true?”**

The question is:

👉 *Do we want systems that reward truth — or systems that reward whatever spreads fastest?*
