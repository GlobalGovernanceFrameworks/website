**What I'd flag:**

**The Proximity Priority metric may conflict with transit metrics.** Section 8.2 targets 70% of population within a 15-minute walk of daily needs by 2040, and simultaneously targets 95% within 15-minute walk of affordable transit. If Proximity Priority succeeds, transit ridership should *decline* in well-designed neighborhoods. The metrics don't acknowledge this tension. You should add a note that declining ridership in high-proximity areas is a success signal, not a failure, and that transit metrics should be evaluated in tandem with proximity metrics.

**Freight section lacks a governance body.** Passenger transport has the GMC. Freight has representatives on the GMC (Section 3.1), but no dedicated freight governance mechanism. Given that freight is roughly half of transport emissions and involves different stakeholders (port authorities, shipping lines, rail freight operators, logistics companies), it may need a standing committee or sub-council — similar to what we just added to the Prometheus Protocol's GPSC.

**The MaaS Commons (6.2) has a platform monopoly risk.** An open-source MaaS platform governed by the Aurora Accord is good in principle, but the document doesn't address what happens when a proprietary platform (Uber, Google Maps) already dominates journey planning in a region. The MaaS Commons needs either an interoperability mandate (proprietary platforms must feed data into the Commons platform) or a strategy for achieving adoption against entrenched incumbents. Without this, it's a technically correct proposal that loses to network effects.

**Transition timeline for freight is ambitious without intermediate targets.** The target of 100% electrified rail freight and sail-freight for ocean shipping by 2035 (Section 5.2) is nine years away. There are no intermediate checkpoints. Phase 1 (2026-2028) mentions "freight-focused" pilots but doesn't specify what's being piloted. This needs Phase 1 and Phase 2 freight-specific targets.

**One small thing:** The "Freiburg's Mobility Transition" case study in Appendix C is a good addition (supports Proximity Priority), but the other case studies are carried over from v7.0. You might want to add a freight-specific case study — Rotterdam's port decarbonization program or the EU's Marco Polo freight shift initiatives would fit.
