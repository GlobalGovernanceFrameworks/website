# 2. The “Cybernetic Firmware” Framing Is Risky

This is brilliant intellectually—but politically dangerous.

EU officials (especially in the European Commission):

* are not used to cybernetic language
* may interpret it as:

  * technocratic overreach
  * loss of democratic legitimacy

---

## Fix:

Keep the idea, soften the language:

Replace:

> “cybernetic firmware update”

With something like:

> **“operational enhancement to subsidiarity implementation”**

You can keep “cybernetic” later in technical annexes.
